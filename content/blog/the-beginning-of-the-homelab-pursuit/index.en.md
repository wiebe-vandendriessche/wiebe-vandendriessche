> **Homelabbing according to Reddit**
>
> A hobby where you pay for the hardware, the electricity and the fan noise so you can self-host apps you could probably have used for free. Success is measured in flashing LEDs and meticulously dressed cables, proudly posted as glamour shots for strangers who will reply that it needs more RAM.
